Loft and roof window fitting in Fulham

A roof window turns a dark loft into a usable room. Fitting one means cutting into the roof, so the structure, the weatherproofing and the inside lining all have to be right.

What we fit

  • Centre-pivot roof windows. The most common type, easy to clean from inside.
  • Top-hung roof windows. Open outwards from the top for a clearer view, and some can act as an escape window.
  • Conservation-style roof windows. Slimmer frames with a central bar, designed for period roofs and conservation areas.
  • Electric and solar windows. Opened by remote control or rain sensor, useful for high-up windows.

What's involved, step by step

1. Survey the roof

We check the roof pitch, the covering (slate or tile), and where the rafters are. The window size is chosen to suit the rafter spacing.

2. Cut the opening

Slates or tiles are stripped around the opening and any rafter that has to be cut is trimmed with new timbers either side, so the roof keeps its strength.

3. Fit the frame and flashing

The frame is fixed to the roof, and a flashing kit made for your roof covering is fitted so rain runs around the window, not into the roof.

4. Make good the roof

Slates or tiles are cut and refitted neatly around the flashing.

5. Line inside

The inside reveals are insulated, boarded and plastered, angled so they let in more light, ready for decorating.

Choosing a roof window? Check these first

  • Your roof pitch. Roof windows are made for set pitch ranges.
  • Your roof covering: slate, plain tile or profiled tile each need a different flashing kit.
  • Whether the room is or will be a bedroom. Loft rooms may need an escape window of a minimum size.
  • If you're in a conservation area, ask about conservation-style windows.

How long does it take?

Replacing a roof window like for like is usually a day. A new opening, including lining inside, usually takes two to three days, weather permitting.

Rules and regulations

Rooflights are often allowed without planning permission on a house, as long as they don't stick out more than 150 mm from the roof or sit higher than the ridge. Conservation areas, listed buildings and flats are different, and planning may be needed, especially on a front roof slope. A new roof window cuts into the structure and changes heat loss, so it comes under the Building Regulations, and replacements are controlled fittings that must be signed off. We check what applies before cutting into your roof.

Questions people ask

Do I need planning permission for a Velux-style window?

Often not on a house, but conservation areas, listed buildings and flats usually change that. We check first.

Will a roof window leak?

Not if it is flashed with the right kit for your roof and the slates or tiles are made good properly. Leaks almost always come from shortcuts with the flashing.

Can a roof window be a fire escape?

Some models are designed as escape windows, with a minimum opening size and height above the floor. We make sure loft bedrooms have one where needed.

Many Fulham and Hammersmith terraces have butterfly or shallow rear roof slopes, which affects which windows fit. Large parts of Kensington and Chelsea are conservation areas, where conservation-style windows and planning checks are often needed.
