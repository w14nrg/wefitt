Skylight fitting in Fulham

Flat roofs over kitchens, back additions and landings are perfect for skylights. The light pours straight down, which makes a dark room feel twice the size.

What we fit

  • Fixed flat rooflights. Frameless-look glass units, the most popular modern choice.
  • Opening rooflights. Electric opening for ventilation, sometimes with rain sensors.
  • Roof lanterns. A pitched glass lantern for a traditional or statement look.
  • Replacing old domes. Swapping tired plastic domes for modern double-glazed units.

What's involved, step by step

1. Check the roof

We look at the flat roof covering, the joists and the room below. An old roof near the end of its life may need re-covering at the same time.

2. Form the opening

The opening is cut and trimmed with new timbers so the joists stay supported.

3. Build the upstand

A timber kerb is built so the rooflight sits well above the roof surface, typically at least 150 mm, keeping standing water away.

4. Waterproof

The roof covering is dressed up the upstand and sealed, using the same system as the roof.

5. Fit the rooflight and line inside

The rooflight is fixed and sealed, then the inside is boarded, plastered and finished.

Choosing a skylight? Check these first

  • Measure the opening you want inside the room, and check where the joists run.
  • Think about heat. A large south-facing rooflight can overheat a room in summer, and solar-control glass helps.
  • Decide whether you need it to open for ventilation.
  • Check the condition of the flat roof. It is easiest to re-cover it at the same time.

How long does it take?

Replacing an existing rooflight is usually a day. A new opening with upstand and lining usually takes two to three days.

Rules and regulations

Rooflights are often permitted development on a house, but not always in conservation areas, on listed buildings or on flats. New openings come under the Building Regulations for structure and heat loss, and replacements are controlled fittings that need to be signed off. We check what applies to yours.

Questions people ask

Will a skylight make my kitchen hot?

It can on a south-facing roof. Solar-control glass and an opening rooflight for ventilation make a big difference.

Can you replace an old plastic dome?

Yes. Old domes are a common job, swapped for a modern double-glazed rooflight on a new upstand.

Can a rooflight go over a landing?

Yes, if there is a flat roof above. It is a great way to light a dark stairwell.
