Stud walls and partitions in Fulham

A stud wall can turn one big room into two, create an en-suite or build a utility cupboard. Done properly it is solid, quiet and indistinguishable from the rest of the house.

What we fit

  • Room dividers. Splitting a large bedroom or creating a home office.
  • En-suite walls. Moisture-resistant board on the wet side.
  • Cupboards and utility spaces. Boxing in boilers, washing machines and storage.
  • Loft partitions. Dwarf walls and partitions in loft rooms.

What's involved, step by step

1. Plan

Where the wall goes, the door position, and what happens to light, heating and sockets in both rooms.

2. Frame

Head plate, sole plate and studs, typically at 400 or 600 mm centres, with noggins where shelves, TVs or basins will be fixed.

3. Services

Cables and pipes run inside the wall before it is closed up.

4. Insulate and board

Mineral wool between the studs and acoustic plasterboard for sound, moisture-resistant board in wet rooms.

5. Skim

Joints taped and the wall skimmed smooth, ready for decorating.

Planning a new wall? Check these first

  • Each new room still needs light, ventilation and heat.
  • A new bedroom needs a safe escape route, which can affect where the door goes.
  • Plan sockets and light switches for both sides.
  • In a flat, check whether the lease needs consent for alterations.

How long does it take?

A simple dividing wall with a door is usually two to three days, plus drying time before decorating.

Rules and regulations

Creating a new room can bring in Building Regulations on fire escape, ventilation and electrics, particularly for bedrooms and in houses of three storeys or more. Non-structural stud walls don't need structural approval, but removing walls is a different job and may.

Questions people ask

Will I hear through a stud wall?

Much less with mineral wool insulation and acoustic plasterboard. It makes a big difference.

Can I hang a TV on a stud wall?

Yes, if we put noggins in the right place while building it. Tell us beforehand.

Can you take a stud wall down?

Yes, non-structural ones. We check it isn't load-bearing first.
