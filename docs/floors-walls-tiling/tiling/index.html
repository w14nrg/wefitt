Wall and floor tiling in Fulham

Good tiling is decided before the first tile goes on: the base it sits on, the adhesive, and where the first line is set out. That is what stops cracks, lippage and thin cuts in the corners.

What we fit

  • Porcelain and ceramic. Hard-wearing and easy to clean, from small metro tiles to large format slabs.
  • Natural stone. Marble, limestone and slate, sealed before and after grouting.
  • Splashbacks. Kitchen splashbacks and behind basins.
  • Victorian and patterned floors. Geometric and encaustic-style floors for halls and paths.

What's involved, step by step

1. Prepare the base

Walls flat and sound. Wet areas on tile backer board and tanked. Timber floors overboarded or fitted with a decoupling mat so movement doesn't crack the tiles.

2. Set out

We find the centre lines and plan the layout so cuts are even on both sides and nothing is a thin sliver at the door.

3. Fix

The right adhesive for the tile and the base, flexible on timber or underfloor heating, with full coverage under large tiles.

4. Grout and seal

Grout to suit the room, stone sealed, and movement joints at edges and changes of material.

5. Finish

Trims, silicone at corners and around baths and trays, and a final clean.

Buying your own tiles? Check these first

  • Order about 10% extra, all from the same batch.
  • Large format tiles need very flat walls and floors. Levelling may be needed.
  • Natural stone needs sealing and some is not suitable for wet floors.
  • Check slip ratings for bathroom and kitchen floors.

How long does it take?

A splashback is usually a day. A full bathroom floor and walls usually takes several days, including drying times.

Questions people ask

Can you tile over old tiles?

Sometimes, if they are sound and flat, but in wet areas and on floors we usually strip back to a proper base.

Why do floor tiles crack?

Usually movement in a timber floor or poor adhesive coverage. A decoupling mat and full bed prevent it.

Do you tile with underfloor heating?

Yes, with flexible adhesive and following the heating maker's instructions.
