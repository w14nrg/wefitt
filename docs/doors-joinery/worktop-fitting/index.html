Worktop fitting in Fulham

New worktops can transform a kitchen without replacing the units. We cut, joint and seal them on site, and get the units ready for stone and quartz templating.

What we fit

  • Laminate. Affordable and hard-wearing, cut and jointed on site.
  • Solid wood. Oak, walnut and others, oiled before and after fitting.
  • Compact laminate. Thin, solid and waterproof right through, cut with special tools.
  • Stone and quartz. Templated, made and fitted by the stone company once we have prepared and levelled the units.

What's involved, step by step

1. Check the units are level

A worktop is only as flat as the units under it. We level and fix them first.

2. Disconnect the sink and hob

Taps and waste come off, and the hob is disconnected. A gas hob must be reconnected by a Gas Safe registered engineer.

3. Cut and joint

Corners are jointed with a mason's mitre or a jointing strip, and the joint is glued and bolted tight from underneath.

4. Cut-outs

Sink and hob holes are cut to the template, with corners radiused so they don't crack.

5. Seal every cut edge

Exposed chipboard or bare wood is sealed, especially around the sink. Water getting into the core is what makes worktops swell.

6. Reconnect and finish

Sink and taps reconnected and tested, upstands or splashback fitted, and the joint to the wall sealed.

Buying your own worktop? Check these first

  • Measure every run and add the overhang. Standard overhang is about 20 mm past the doors.
  • Solid wood needs oiling on all faces before fitting. Leaving the underside bare lets it cup.
  • Undermount sinks need a worktop made for them, usually stone, quartz, compact or solid surface.
  • Check the hob maker's minimum distances from the worktop edge and walls.

How long does it take?

Laminate or wood worktops for a typical kitchen usually take one to two days. Stone is templated after the units are ready and fitted a week or two later by the supplier.

Rules and regulations

Gas hobs can only be disconnected and reconnected by a Gas Safe registered engineer.

Questions people ask

Can you change my worktops without a new kitchen?

Yes, as long as the units are sound and level. It is one of the most cost-effective kitchen updates.

Can a laminate worktop have an undermount sink?

Not usually. Undermount sinks leave the cut edge exposed, so they need a material that is waterproof all the way through.

How do I look after a wood worktop?

Oil it regularly, especially around the sink, and wipe up standing water.

Worktops in Fulham, Hammersmith, Kensington and Chelsea

Kitchens in Fulham and Hammersmith back additions are long and narrow, so worktop joints and runs are planned carefully. In Kensington and Chelsea flats, getting long worktops up stairs or into a lift decides where the joints go.
