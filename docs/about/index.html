About us

Same team, same shop

We Fitt is the fitting side of En-Suites & Bathrooms. The shop at 287 Munster Road, the people who answer the phone and the team who turn up are all the same.

Photo of NicholasA photo of Nicholas in the shop goes here.

Why We Fitt?

People walked past the shop and asked if we could fit a door, a tap, a kitchen they'd already bought. The answer was always yes. En-Suites & Bathrooms does full renovations; We Fitt is for everything else, from a single job to a whole kitchen.

Nicholas

Nicholas has lived in Kensington all his life and has worked in the plumbing and building trade since 1998. He grew up in a building family and was on site as a young boy, learning to plaster. Customers deal with him directly.

The team

Carpenters, plumbers, electricians, a plasterer, a decorator and a tiler, all on our own team, so one call covers the whole job. Each trade does its own part, and nobody leaves a job half-finished for someone else to sort out.

Come and see us

The shop is on Munster Road in Fulham. Come in, bring photos or measurements, and talk the job through. Opening hours and directions.
