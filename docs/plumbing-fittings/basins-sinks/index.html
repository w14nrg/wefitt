Basin and sink fitting in Fulham

A basin has to be fixed solid, sit level and drain properly. Most problems we're called to, like wobbling basins, slow drains and leaks under the unit, come from shortcuts at fitting.

What we fit

  • Wall-hung basins. Need solid fixings. On a stud wall, timber noggins are fitted behind the board.
  • Pedestal basins. Classic basins on a pedestal that hides the pipes.
  • Vanity units. Basin set into a cabinet for storage.
  • Countertop basins. Bowls sitting on a worktop, with a tall tap or wall tap.
  • Kitchen sinks. Inset stainless, undermount, and ceramic Belfast sinks on a support unit.

What's involved, step by step

1. Check the waste

Where the waste pipe is and how it will meet the new trap. Lining these up stops strain on the joints.

2. Fixings

Basin brackets or vanity fixed into solid wall or noggins. Plasterboard alone won't hold a basin.

3. Tap and waste first

The tap and waste are fitted to the basin before it goes on the wall, which is far easier and tighter.

4. Connect

Supplies with isolation valves, trap connected, everything level.

5. Seal and test

Sealed to the wall, then filled and drained to check for leaks.

Buying a basin or sink? Check these first

  • Check the number of tap holes matches your tap.
  • Basins without an overflow need a waste without one too.
  • Belfast sinks are heavy and need a dedicated support unit.
  • Undermount kitchen sinks need a stone, quartz, compact or solid surface worktop.

How long does it take?

Swapping a basin or sink is usually half a day. Vanity units and Belfast sinks with unit changes take longer.

Questions people ask

My basin wobbles. Can you fix it?

Usually, by re-fixing into proper fixings and adding support. On stud walls we may need to add a noggin.

Can you fit a vanity unit I bought online?

Yes. Send us the link and we'll check it works with your waste position.

Can you swap my kitchen sink without changing the worktop?

Often, if the new sink is the same size or bigger. Smaller sinks may not cover the old cut-out.
