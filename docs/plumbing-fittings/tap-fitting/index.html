Tap fitting in Fulham

Fitting a tap looks like a ten-minute job. What decides whether it works well is matching it to your water pressure, and fitting it so it can be serviced without turning off the whole house.

What we fit

  • Kitchen mixer taps. Single-lever, pull-out spray and filter taps.
  • Boiling water taps. Need cupboard space for the tank, a socket and often a filter.
  • Basin taps. Monobloc, pillar or wall-mounted with a concealed valve.
  • Bath taps. Deck-mounted, bath-shower mixers and wall-mounted fillers.
  • Outside taps. With a double check valve to protect your drinking water.

What's involved, step by step

1. Check your pressure

Many modern taps need high pressure from a combi boiler or unvented cylinder. On a gravity system with a tank in the loft, we fit low-pressure rated taps so they don't dribble.

2. Isolate and remove

Water isolated, the old tap removed and the tap hole cleaned up.

3. Fit isolation valves

Small valves on the hot and cold feeds, so the tap can be serviced without turning off the house.

4. Fit and connect

Tap fixed tight so it won't twist, connected with flexible tails or solid pipe.

5. Test

Run hot and cold, check every joint for drips, and check the flow.

Buying a new tap? Check these first

  • Check the minimum working pressure on the box against your system.
  • Check the number of tap holes in your sink or basin.
  • Check the spout reaches the middle of the bowl and clears the back of the sink.
  • Buy taps approved for UK water supplies (look for WRAS approval).

How long does it take?

Most tap swaps take an hour or two. Boiling water taps, wall-mounted taps and replacing old pipework take longer.

Rules and regulations

Taps and fittings must comply with the Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations, which is why we recommend WRAS-approved taps. Outside taps need a double check valve to stop water flowing back into the mains.

Questions people ask

My new tap only dribbles. Why?

Usually it's a high-pressure tap on a low-pressure system, or a blocked aerator or valve. We can check which.

Can you fit a boiling water tap?

Yes, as long as there is space for the tank in the cupboard and a socket nearby.

Can you fix a dripping tap instead of replacing it?

Often, yes. A new washer or cartridge fixes most drips.
