Mirror and bathroom cabinet fitting in Fulham

Mirrors and cabinets are heavy, usually go up on tiled walls, and the illuminated ones need power. We fix them safely without cracking your tiles.

What we fit

  • Plain and framed mirrors. Fixed on hidden brackets or cleats.
  • Illuminated and demister mirrors. LED lighting and heated pads to stop steaming up, wired by our electrician.
  • Mirror cabinets. Storage behind the mirror, often with lights and a shaver socket.
  • Recessed cabinets. Set into a stud wall so they sit flush.

What's involved, step by step

1. Find what's in the wall

We check for pipes and cables before drilling, especially above basins.

2. Choose the fixings

Solid wall plugs for masonry, or proper cavity fixings or noggins for stud walls.

3. Drill through tiles carefully

Tile bits at low speed, with tape to stop slipping, so tiles don't crack.

4. Hang level

Fixed on brackets, levelled, and checked against the basin and tap.

5. Wire illuminated mirrors

Connected to a suitable supply, tested and certified.

Buying a mirror or cabinet? Check these first

  • Check the weight and the fixings it comes with. Plasterboard fixings are rarely enough.
  • Illuminated mirrors need a power supply. Plan this before tiling if you can.
  • Recessed cabinets need a stud wall deep enough.
  • Check the IP rating if it will be near a bath or shower.

How long does it take?

Hanging a plain mirror or cabinet is usually an hour or two. Illuminated and recessed units take longer.

Rules and regulations

Wiring an illuminated mirror or shaver socket in a bathroom is electrical work in a special location, and is notifiable under Part P where it involves new wiring in the zones around a bath or shower. The mirror must have a suitable IP rating for where it goes.

Questions people ask

Can you fit an illuminated mirror where there was a plain one?

Yes, if we can get power to it. Without chasing into tiles that can mean running a cable from a nearby circuit.

Will drilling crack my tiles?

Not with the right bits and care. Large-format porcelain is the trickiest and we take extra care with it.

Can a cabinet be recessed into the wall?

Into a stud wall, yes. Solid walls are much harder.
