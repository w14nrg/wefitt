Radiator and towel rail fitting in Fulham

A new radiator has to be the right size for the room and connect to your pipes without leaks. Swapping one also means draining, refilling and re-treating the heating system.

What we fit

  • Panel radiators. Modern, efficient and quick to swap.
  • Column radiators. The Victorian look, heavy, so wall fixings matter.
  • Designer and vertical radiators. For narrow walls in halls and kitchens.
  • Heated towel rails. On the central heating, electric, or dual fuel to work all year.

What's involved, step by step

1. Size for the room

We work out the heat output the room needs. Too small and the room stays cold.

2. Drain down

The system is drained or isolated, and the old radiator removed.

3. Fix and connect

Brackets fixed into solid wall, valves fitted, pipes adjusted if the centres are different.

4. Refill and treat

The system is refilled, bled and dosed with inhibitor to protect it from corrosion. Combi systems are re-pressurised.

5. Balance

Valves adjusted so every radiator gets its share of heat.

Buying a radiator? Check these first

  • Measure the pipe centres of your old radiator. A matching size means a cleaner swap.
  • Check the heat output in watts suits the room.
  • Column radiators are heavy. Check the wall can take the brackets.
  • Electric towel rails in a bathroom must be suitable for that zone.

How long does it take?

A like-for-like radiator swap is usually a couple of hours. Moving a radiator or changing several takes a day or more.

Rules and regulations

Fitting an electric or dual-fuel towel rail in a bathroom is electrical work in a special location, and is notifiable under Part P where it involves new wiring in the zones around a bath or shower.

Questions people ask

Can you move a radiator to another wall?

Yes, by extending the pipes, usually under the floorboards.

Will a towel rail heat my bathroom?

Many towel rails give out less heat than a radiator. In larger bathrooms we check the output is enough.

Do I need new valves?

Usually, yes. Thermostatic radiator valves let you control each room.
